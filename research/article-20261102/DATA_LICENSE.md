# Dataset provenance and licence

UCI Forest Fires, Cortez and Morais (2007), https://doi.org/10.24432/C5D88D, is distributed under CC BY 4.0. The original CSV has 517 records and SHA-256 0d6586a1fa52f55bef48578aef14eb97273f1e9330e1a53423df497a77065253. Four exact duplicate rows were removed for modelling; the original file is retained. X/Y are the original discrete cell labels; no metric cell dimensions are inferred.

All predictions and scores are computational outputs from the included protocol, not additional measured fires. Figures are analytical visualisations of that data. The MIT software licence does not override the dataset licence.
